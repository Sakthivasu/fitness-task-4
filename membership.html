Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

Can I cancel my membership anytime? ▼
Yes, you can cancel your membership at any time with no penalty through your member portal or by contacting support.
Do you offer a free trial? ▼
Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for new members to explore our facilities, classes, and expert trainers.
What are the payment options? ▼
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking for monthly and annual membership billing.
Is there a joining fee? ▼
No, there are zero joining fees when you sign up for any of our standard membership plans.
Are personal trainers included? ▼
Personal trainer support is included in Pro and Premium plans. Basic members can book sessions separately.
