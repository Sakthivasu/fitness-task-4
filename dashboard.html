Terms & Conditions

Terms for using Pulse Athletics memberships, facilities, and services.

1. Membership & Billing

Memberships are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis depending on the plan selected. Cancellation requests should be submitted before the next billing cycle.

2. Facility Rules & Safety

Members must follow facility safety protocols, wear appropriate attire, and treat other members and staff respectfully. Rule violations may result in account suspension.

3. Exercise & Liability

Physical exercise and sports involve inherent risks. Members should choose activities appropriate to their health and ability and seek professional medical advice when needed.

Privacy

This static demo stores profile details, bookings, and workout logs in your browser using local storage. This information is not transmitted to a server.

Cookies

This demo does not set tracking cookies. Browser local storage is used to remember your dashboard data on this device.
